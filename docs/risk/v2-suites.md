# Risk: V2, suites per tenant

The task touched these requirements (classes from `docs/trace/risk.md`):

| Requirement | Class | How it was touched |
|---|---|---|
| TF-244 Suites per tenant | critical (Tenancy and control plane) | Built: the eProcurement Suite declared with its skeleton module, a unit's composition from its configuration, plans per suite, suites added and removed by update jobs, the About page |
| TF-001 Tenant registry | critical (Tenancy and control plane) | A tenant is registered with its suites and their plans in one transaction, with the operator's optional reason; its record names its suites |
| TF-002 Provisioning | critical (Tenancy and control plane) | Provisioning installs the tenant's suites: the migrate, seed and unit steps get them in the unit's environment |
| TF-030 Permission catalogue | critical (Authorization) | Two permissions: `purchasing.suite.read` (low, in a unit that runs the suite) and `tenancy.suites.manage` (critical, in the control plane); a suite's permissions are in the system roles only while the unit runs it |
| TF-218 Validation support for regulated tenants | critical (Regulated-industry controls) | The installation record names the unit's suites and checks its seeds against the image's seeds for its composition |
| TF-040 Module and feature entitlements | high (Entitlements) | Each entry carries its group and its own plan's grace days; the document lists the suites with their plans |
| TF-042 Plans and plan templates | high (Entitlements) | A plan belongs to the Foundation or one suite and names only that group's codes; a suite's starter plans are seeded from the catalogue |
| TF-043 Entitlement checks in UI and API | high (Entitlements) | `/me/context` names the suites the unit runs; the home page shows a suite's group only where it is installed and entitled |
| TF-045 Entitlement audit and history | high (Entitlements) | Adding and removing a suite write a history row per change with the operator's reason, and audit events of their own |
| TF-210 Per-tenant deployment units | high (Fleet operations) | The unit's environment carries `Suites__Installed`, so a change of suites recreates its containers |
| TF-172 Accessibility | high (UI shell and design system) | The About page and the home page with the suite's group are scanned in both schemes |

The change is classed **critical**.

**What could go wrong.** A tenant that did not buy the suite could get its schema, its endpoints, its seeds, its permissions or its shell modules. A unit could keep running a removed suite, because its containers were not recreated, or run a suite the control plane does not record. Removing a suite could drop its schema or delete its rows or files, so adding it again would not find them. A plan of the eProcurement Suite could grant another suite's module or a Foundation feature, or applying a suite's plan could change the Foundation's entitlements, such as taking role export away. A suite's module could keep working past its own plan's grace, or stop early, because the wrong plan's grace days were applied. A suite could be added or removed without an audit event naming the operator and the reason, or half-way: entitlements changed with no job to install the suite, or a job with no entitlements. The installation record could vouch for a unit whose seeds differ from the image's for its suites, or fail a unit that removed a suite and kept its schema. Standard and Enterprise could change meaning when the plans gained a group.

**What proves it does not.** A unit's composition is the Foundation and the suites its configuration names: the host test runs the API host without and with `Suites:Installed` and checks the modules, the catalogue and the route, which answers 404 without the suite (`HostTests`); a suite name the image does not carry stops the unit, and `AddModules` refuses a suite's module that was not named (`SuiteInstallationTests`). Only a unit that runs the suite migrates its schema, its seed records the installation with the job and the release and audits it, and a unit that stops running it keeps the schema and the row, which the suite finds again with its first installation kept (`SuiteInstallationTests`, on PostgreSQL). In a unit's API, the About page answers with the installation, the plan and the module; the context names the suite; a person without the permission is refused; past the Foundation plan's grace and within the suite plan's the module still works, and past its own it is refused with `entitlements.module_disabled`; a unit without the suite answers 404 and names neither the suite nor its module (`AboutEndpointTests`). In the control plane, adding a suite applies only its group's plan, requests an update job and audits `tenancy.suite_added` with the operator and the reason; a suite plan applied to the Foundation's group, a second add, an unknown suite and a missing reason are refused; the store's document is the union with each entry's own grace; a suite plan switching its module to read only leaves the Foundation's rows as they were; removing it takes only its rows, with history rows carrying the reason, audits `tenancy.suite_removed`, and adding it again gives them back; a tenant is created with its suites or refused whole (`SuiteTests`). A plan naming another group's module or feature is refused when saved and when applied, naming the code and its group, including between two suites (`SuiteTests`, TF-244 AC7). The fleet passes the suites to every command and to the unit, the seed gets the job's id, and adding or removing a suite is an update that recreates the unit with or without it and removes nothing (`ProvisioningTests`, `ComposeUnitRuntimeTests`). The document reads as before without groups and grace per entry (`EntitlementRulesTests`). The installation record checks a unit with the suite against the image's seeds for `procurement`, fails it against the Foundation's, reports a removed suite's schema as kept, and fails a unit with a suite when the image states no seeds per composition (`installation_record_test.py`). The shell shows the suite's group only for an installed suite (`registry.test.ts`, `HomePage.test.tsx`), the About page in words (`AboutSuitePage.test.tsx`), and the scan finds no WCAG 2.1 AA violation on either page in either scheme (`suite-about.spec.ts`). Standard and Enterprise became Foundation plans by the migration's default, and every test of S1 to V1 that applies them still passes.

**Results.** make verify passed on its second run; the first failed the shell's placement test under coverage only, because Vitest's module cache on the laptop held a transform of it from before the suite's folder existed (CI installs afresh; STATUS has the line). 1255 .NET test cases (962 methods), 414 Vitest, 158 scans, 96 Python; the gate with nothing failing but the one approved exception (497 licences); coverage 90.54% and 85.54% against floors of 90 and 85; the matrix: TF-244 7 of 7 criteria with a test (AC1 17, AC2 10, AC3 4, AC4 4, AC5 4, AC6 2, AC7 2), 28 tests name ADR-0059; 86 of 500 requirements and 296 of 1748 criteria with a test, S1 to S3 done with 31 exceptions and none missing; 7 untraced. On the laptop, on the branch's images: the control plane and the fleet from them; echo given the suite by `make suite-add`, whose update job migrated `procurement_purchasing`, seeded the installation with its job and recreated the unit with `Suites__Installed=procurement`; its About page answered 200 with the plan, the job and Purchasing enabled, and the context named the suite. Bravo, updated without the suite, answered 404 `api.route.not_found` and had no schema; given the suite, it answered 200 with its own job; with the suite removed, it answered 404 again, its system roles no longer held `purchasing.suite.read`, and its schema and installation row were where they were. The control plane's trail holds `tenancy.suite_added` for echo and bravo and `tenancy.suite_removed` for bravo, each with the operator, the reason, the plan and the job. Acme updated without the suite. The smoke 26 of 26 on echo, its `suites` step reading the About page; the browser tests 44 of 44 on echo, the suite's test opening the About page from the home page, after the Subscription page's test learned that a tenant with the suite has a licensed module.

**What is left.** The seeds' hashes grow with the subsets of the carried suites; revisit with a third suite (ADR-0059). A removed suite's data stays without end, and deleting it belongs to a tenant's offboarding, a later decision. The suite's definition moves out of the Purchasing module when the suite's products arrive.
